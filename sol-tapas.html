Our Story

Where tradition meets innovation

SOL is the culinary vision of Chef Mate Janković — a restaurant that redefines the tapas concept through the lens of Croatian heritage. Situated on the first floor of the historic Johann Franck building, overlooking Zagreb's iconic Ban Josip Jelačić Square.

Every dish is a conversation between memory and imagination — familiar Croatian ingredients transformed into small, exquisite bites that invite you to explore, share, and discover.

Our Philosophy

The SOL concept

01

Croatian Heritage, Modern Spirit

SOL draws from the rich tapestry of Croatian regional cuisine — the Dalmatian coast, Istrian truffles, Slavonian traditions, Lika's mountains — and presents them in a tapas format that encourages sharing, discovery, and conversation. The menu is divided into cold, warm, and sweet tapas, with a special tasting set for two.

Locally Sourced, Artfully Crafted

We source exclusively Croatian products: cheese from the island of Pag, prosciutto from Dalmatia, truffles from Istria, wine from across the country's diverse wine regions. Our craft drinks — lemonades, fresh juices, and cocktails — are crafted in-house with the same devotion as our food.

A View Worth Remembering

From our tables you can see Zagreb's main square from a perspective that few ever discover. The intimate space, warm lighting, and attentive service create an atmosphere that is both relaxed and refined — equally suited to a business lunch or a romantic evening.
